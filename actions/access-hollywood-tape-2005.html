“Grab ’Em by the Pussy” Tape (Access Hollywood, 2005)

In October 2016, one month before the presidential election, The Washington Post released a 2005 “Access Hollywood” recording in which Donald Trump bragged about using his celebrity status to commit sexual assault. On the tape, Trump told host Billy Bush that when you are famous, “you can do anything,” adding: “Grab ’em by the pussy. You can do anything.”

The footage captured Trump describing his attempts to forcibly kiss and grope women without consent, framing the behavior as something he felt entitled to because of his fame. The release of the tape triggered widespread condemnation, including from members of Trump’s own party, and prompted dozens of Republican officials to rescind endorsements or call for him to drop out of the race.

Trump dismissed the comments as “locker room talk,” but numerous women subsequently came forward describing similar behavior consistent with what he boasted about on the tape. Despite the uproar, Trump remained the Republican nominee and went on to win the 2016 election.

The incident continues to be one of the most widely cited examples of Trump’s history of misogyny and his own public acknowledgment of non-consensual sexual conduct.
