Trump Posts Racist Video of the Obamas

February 2026

On February 6, 2026, President Donald Trump posted a racist video on his Truth Social account depicting former President Barack Obama and former first lady Michelle Obama as apes. Trump removed the post after approximately twelve hours, denied responsibility for it, and refused to apologize.

The depiction of Black people as apes is one of the oldest and most widely recognized racist tropes in American history, historically used to dehumanize Black Americans and to justify violence, including lynchings. Douglas Brinkley, a presidential historian, told the New York Times that the video was “just hard-boiled racism using the oldest trope against Black people imaginable.”

When questioned by reporters, Trump said, “I didn’t make a mistake.” White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt dismissed the post as an “internet meme” and told reporters to “stop the fake outrage and report on something today that actually matters to the American public.”

Several senior Republicans publicly condemned the post. Senator Tim Scott of South Carolina, head of the National Republican Senatorial Committee, called it “the most racist thing I’ve seen out of this White House.” Representative Mike Lawler of New York described it as “wrong and incredibly offensive.” Representative Michael R. Turner of Ohio said the “racist images” were “offensive, heart breaking, and unacceptable.” Senator Roger Wicker of Mississippi said the president “should take it down and apologize.”
