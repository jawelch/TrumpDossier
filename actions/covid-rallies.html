Campaigning During COVID, Leading to an Estimated 700+ Deaths

2020

During the COVID-19 pandemic, President Trump held many large campaign rallies with his supporters against the warnings of medical experts. Researchers from Stanford's Institute for Economic Policy Research studied 18 meetings held by the Trump campaign in 2020. The study concluded that the events resulted in more than 30,000 incremental confirmed cases of COVID, and likely more than 700 deaths.

Public health officials discouraged the events and warned of additional infections. Some of the rallies were held indoors. Trump's crass decision-making and ignorance tangibly intensified a public health crisis. His insistence to put on a vanity show in the midst of a pandemic directly led to hundreds of people dying, if not more.
