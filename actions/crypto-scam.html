Trump Crypto Coin Makes $1.4 Billion, Investors Lose $3.8 Billion

2025-2026

In the first year of his second term, President Trump reported over $1.4 billion in income from his family's crypto ventures. Trump and his family own a crypto company named World Liberty Financial which reported almost $800 million in profits. Trump's official meme coin netted him over $600 million. Since the launch of his crypto businesses, almost a million American investors have lost an estimated $3.8 billion, with some estimating the number to be as high as $7 billion.

The transfer of billions of dollars from American citizens to the sitting president through a risky vessel like a 'meme' coin is simply corrupt. His use of policy to advance an industry he is heavily invested in is criminal. Trump made the following official actions to bolster the crypto market:

  • Executive Order on Digital Assets (Executive Order 14178): Declared cryptocurrency and blockchain innovation a national priority, established a federal crypto policy working group, and prohibited development of a U.S. central bank digital currency.
  • Strategic Bitcoin Reserve (Executive Order 14233): Created a federal reserve of Bitcoin and other digital assets using cryptocurrency already seized by the government.
  • SEC Leadership and Regulatory Shift (Presidential Appointments and SEC Policy Changes): Appointed crypto-friendly regulators who reduced enforcement actions against the industry and prioritized developing clearer regulatory rules.
  • GENIUS Act (Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act): Signed the first major federal cryptocurrency law, establishing a regulatory framework for payment stablecoins.
  • Reduced DOJ Crypto Enforcement (Department of Justice Policy Directive): Scaled back Justice Department enforcement efforts by disbanding a specialized crypto crime unit and narrowing prosecution priorities.
  • Promotion of Crypto Innovation (SEC Regulatory Initiatives): Directed regulators to encourage blockchain-based financial products, including tokenized securities and regulatory exemptions for new crypto businesses.
