Former Trump Officials Warn of “Absolute, Unchecked Power” in a Second Trump Term

October 2024

In October 2024, thirteen former Trump administration officials publicly backed retired Marine Gen. John Kelly’s warnings about the dangers of a second Trump presidency. In a joint letter, they stated that Donald Trump seeks “absolute, unchecked power” and emphasized that his “disdain for the American military and admiration for dictators like Hitler is rooted in his desire” for that power.

The group urged voters to take Kelly’s concerns seriously, writing: “For the good of our country, our democracy, and our Constitution, we are asking you to listen closely and carefully to General Kelly’s warning.”

The signatories spanned multiple sectors of Trump’s former governing coalition, including senior White House staff, national security officials, and Homeland Security personnel.

Key figures included former White House press secretary Stephanie Grisham and former Pence adviser Olivia Troye. Other notable signers were former White House communications director Anthony Scaramucci; Pence communications director Alyssa Farah Griffin; Brooke Vosburgh Alexander, a top aide in the Commerce Department; National Security Council officials Mark Harvey and Peter Jennison; former deputy press secretary Sarah Matthews; and former U.S. ambassador to Micronesia Robert Riley.

Three former Homeland Security officials — Kevin Carroll, Elizabeth Neumann, and Sofia Kinzinger (the wife of former Rep. Adam Kinzinger) — also joined the letter, underscoring a particularly sharp rebuke from within Trump’s own national-security sphere.

The letter followed public remarks by Gen. Kelly, who had served as Trump’s White House chief of staff, and who warned that Trump met the definition of a fascist and posed a danger to constitutional governance. The collective statement marked one of the most direct and coordinated warnings issued by former senior officials from within Trump’s own administration.
