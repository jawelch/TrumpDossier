FUNDRAISING BASED ON FALSE ELECTION CLAIMS

Between November 2020 and January 2021, Donald Trump raised hundreds of millions of dollars from supporters through messaging that claimed the money was needed to “defend election integrity.” Investigators found that most funds were directed to political committees under Trump’s control, not legal challenges.

How the Laws May Apply

18 U.S.C. § 1343 — Wire Fraud
Soliciting money using knowingly false statements may constitute wire fraud.

18 U.S.C. § 371 — Conspiracy to Defraud the United States
Coordinating such fundraising while knowing the claims were false may qualify as conspiracy to defraud the United States.
