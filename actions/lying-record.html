Lying Record

2021

According to the Washington Post in January of 2021, President Trump made 30,573 false or misleading claims throughout his first presidential term. No other individual in history has been recorded lying to such an extent.

VICE PRESIDENT VANCE COMPARES TRUMP TO HITLER (2016): During Trump’s first campaign for president in the 2016 Election, now-vice president JD Vance compared Trump to Hitler in a private Facebook message: "I go back and forth between thinking Trump is a cynical asshole like Nixon who wouldn't be that bad (and might even prove useful) or that he's America's Hitler." Vance, at the time a 32-year old author of the best selling memoir Hillbilly Elegy, also publicly described Trump as an “idiot” and “reprehensible.”
