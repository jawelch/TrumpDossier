Trump Campaign Breaks Federal Law Filming in Arlington Cemetery, Physically Accosts Cemetery Employee

August 2024

During the 2024 presidential campaign, two senior Trump campaign aides were involved in a physical altercation with staff at Arlington National Cemetery after illegally recording campaign-related content on cemetery grounds. The incident occurred during a wreath-laying ceremony attended by former President Donald Trump.

Arlington National Cemetery officials had communicated in advance to campaign representatives that photography or video recording for political purposes was prohibited. Despite these warnings, members of the campaign filmed content for use in campaign materials.

According to an official police report later released by the Army, a cemetery employee attempted to enforce federal regulations by instructing campaign staff to stop recording. She was reportedly verbally abused and physically pushed aside by Trump’s deputy campaign manager, Justin Caporale, and Michel Picard, a member of Trump’s advance team.

Arlington National Cemetery released the following statement:

“Federal law prohibits political campaign or election-related activities within Army National Military Cemeteries, to include photographers, content creators or any other persons attending for purposes, or in direct support of a partisan political candidate’s campaign. Arlington National Cemetery reinforced and widely shared this law and its prohibitions with all participants.”

Relevant Federal Law

Political campaign activity on the grounds of Arlington National Cemetery is prohibited under federal regulations governing Army National Military Cemeteries:

  • 32 C.F.R. § 553 – Department of the Army regulations governing conduct at Army National Military Cemeteries.
  • 32 C.F.R. § 553.32 – Prohibits partisan political activities, demonstrations, and campaign-related events on cemetery property.
  • 38 U.S.C. § 2413 – Prohibits unauthorized demonstrations and political activity in national cemeteries.
  • 18 U.S.C. § 111 – Prohibits assaulting, resisting, or impeding certain federal employees while engaged in official duties.
  • 18 U.S.C. § 1752 – Restricts unlawful entry and disorderly conduct in restricted federal buildings or grounds (potentially applicable depending on designation at the time of the event).

The Army’s publicly released report concluded that campaign personnel violated cemetery regulations. The physical contact alleged in the police report raised additional legal questions regarding interference with federal employees performing official duties.

The incident drew bipartisan criticism, with veterans’ organizations emphasizing that Arlington National Cemetery is a nonpartisan memorial site honoring fallen service members and is not to be used for campaign activity.
