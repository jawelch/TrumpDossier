Trump’s Support for North Carolina Lieutenant Governor Mark Robinson (2024)

March 2024

In March 2024, Donald Trump officially endorsed Mark Robinson’s campaign for governor of North Carolina. Trump called Robinson “Martin Luther King on steroids” and stated, “I think you are Martin Luther King times two.”

Robinson’s campaign and public record have generated concern and condemnation, including from some within his own party. Many of the issues critics cite relate to past statements and behavior that opponents describe as deeply offensive, discriminatory, or evidence of poor judgment.

According to a 2024 report from The Washington Post, messages allegedly posted by Robinson between 2008 and 2012 on an adult-website forum described himself as a “Black NAZI,” praised slavery, described peeping on women in public showers, and referenced attraction to transgender pornography.

Robinson has also made repeated inflammatory remarks targeting LGBTQ+ people, calling homosexuality and transgender identity “filth,” and opposing trans rights and anti-discrimination measures. His rhetoric has been covered by CBS News and Them.

In a 2021 church sermon, Robinson used derisive and hostile language about LGBTQ+ people, prompting condemnation from civil rights groups, according to reporting by Newsweek.

He has publicly expressed skepticism toward widely accepted historical events and mainstream science, including questioning official narratives of the 9/11 attacks and dismissing climate-change research as “junk science,” as reported by The Associated Press.

Robinson’s past also includes reported financial and nonprofit-accountability issues. According to reporting, a nonprofit run by his wife owed state money for disallowed expenses, and Robinson has faced claims involving evictions, bankruptcy filings, and failure to file federal income taxes in earlier years.
