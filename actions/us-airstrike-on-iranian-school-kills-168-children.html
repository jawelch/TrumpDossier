U.S. Airstrike on Iranian School Kills 168+ Children

March 2026

In March 2026, an airstrike in Iran destroyed a school building, with local officials and rescue workers reporting that at least 168 children were killed and many others injured. The strike occurred during a series of U.S. military operations targeting Iranian naval infrastructure and other military sites in the region. Local reports indicated that the school was in session when the building was hit.

Early reporting from Iranian authorities and regional media described the strike as one of the deadliest attacks on a civilian educational facility in recent decades. Photographs and video from the aftermath showed the school structure largely collapsed, with rescue workers searching through debris for survivors.

According to reporting by Reuters and The New York Times, preliminary findings from a U.S. investigation indicated that the strike was likely carried out by American forces during the broader military operation. The investigation was ongoing as casualty figures and details surrounding the strike continued to be verified.

U.S. officials said the military operations in the area were aimed at Iranian naval infrastructure and other military targets. The White House initially denied knowledge of any strike on the school and said it was reviewing the circumstances surrounding the incident. Officials later acknowledged that the attack may have been connected to American military activity in the area, though responsibility and targeting decisions were still under investigation.

If confirmed, the incident would represent one of the deadliest single U.S. military strikes involving civilian children in the 21st century. Human rights groups and international observers called for an independent investigation into how the strike occurred and whether civilian protections under international humanitarian law were violated.
