Vice President Vance Compared Trump to Hitler (2016)

2016

During the 2016 presidential campaign, JD Vance — now Vice President of the United States — privately compared Donald Trump to Adolf Hitler. In a Facebook message written at the time, Vance stated: “I go back and forth between thinking Trump is a cynical asshole like Nixon who wouldn't be that bad (and might even prove useful) or that he's America's Hitler.”

At the time, Vance was a 32-year-old author whose memoir Hillbilly Elegy had become a national bestseller. He was not yet an elected official and was publicly critical of Trump during the 2016 campaign.

In addition to the private message, Vance publicly described Trump in 2016 as an “idiot” and “reprehensible.” His statements reflected a broader faction of Republican critics who opposed Trump during his first campaign.

Years later, Vance reversed his position, endorsed Trump, and ultimately became his running mate. The earlier comments resurfaced publicly after the announcement of the ticket, drawing renewed scrutiny given Vance’s current role in the administration.
