Corruption Dossier

Donald Trump's career and effect on the politics of the United States will forever leave a blemish on the tapestry of American history. His record of political, financial, and moral corruption is so overwhelming the American electorate can be forgiven for failing to maintain a precise recollection of it all. The purpose of this site is to create a full account of every unjustifiable infraction of the American spirit committed by the President and his administration. Though his crimes and sins have been well documented through various media organizations, non-profits, judicial, and federal records, this site provides a centralized collection for the ease of the reader.
